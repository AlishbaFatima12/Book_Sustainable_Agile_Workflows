# Welcome to The Secret Life of AI Creatures

![A vibrant, magical forest teeming with glowing creatures of all shapes and sizes](https://i.imgur.com/3g25E0f.png)

This is a book for kids and teens who want to understand the magical world of Artificial Intelligence (AI).

Join Pip, a small, curious creature, on his journey through the Whispering Woods as he discovers the secrets of AI.

## About the Book

This book explains complex AI concepts through simple stories and magical creatures. You will learn about:

*   **Data**: The memories of the forest.
*   **Algorithms**: The magical paths to solve problems.
*   **Machine Learning**: How to teach a stone golem to think.
*   **Neural Networks**: The hidden decisions that shape the world.
*   **Generative AI**: The magic of creating something entirely new.
