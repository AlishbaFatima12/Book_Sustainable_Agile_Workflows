# The Secret Life of AI Creatures Constitution

## Core Principles

### I. Simple and Accessible Language
All content must be written in simple, accessible language suitable for a 9–17 age group. The text should be beginner-friendly, avoiding complex jargon wherever possible and explaining necessary technical terms clearly.

### II. Blend of Fiction and Science
Each chapter must blend imaginative storytelling with accurate, simplified explanations of core AI concepts. The narrative should be the primary vehicle for education, making learning feel magical and engaging.

### III. Rich Visual Storytelling
The book must be rich in colorful, auto-generated diagrams and illustrations to aid understanding. Visuals are not decorative; they are a core part of the storytelling and educational experience.

### IV. Concise and Structured Chapters
The book will consist of exactly 5 chapters, each between 400 and 500 words. This structure ensures the content is digestible and respects the reader's attention span.

### V. Deployable MkDocs Output
The final output must be a complete and deployable MkDocs Material website. All markdown, configuration, and assets should be structured correctly for immediate GitHub Pages deployment.

## Development Workflow

All work will follow a Spec-Driven Development (SDD) process. Specifications will be defined for each chapter, outlining the story, the AI concepts to be covered, and the required illustrations before implementation begins.

## Governance
This constitution is the authoritative guide for the project. All contributions, reviews, and decisions must align with these principles. Amendments require team consensus and must be documented with a clear rationale.
