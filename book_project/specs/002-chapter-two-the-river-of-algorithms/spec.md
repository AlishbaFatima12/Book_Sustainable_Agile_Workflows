# Feature Specification: Chapter 2 - The River of Algorithms

**Feature Branch**: `002-chapter-two-the-river-of-algorithms`
**Created**: 2025-11-26
**Status**: Draft

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - Learning to Navigate (Priority: P1)

As a young reader, I want to learn with Pip how to follow a set of steps to achieve a goal, so I can understand the basic idea of an "algorithm".

**Why this priority**: This chapter introduces the core concept of algorithms, which is fundamental to AI.

**Independent Test**: The chapter can be read on its own and successfully introduces the concept of an algorithm using the metaphor of a river with currents.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1.  **Given** a reader starting the chapter, **When** they read about the River of Algorithms, **Then** they should understand that the currents are like a set of instructions.
2.  **Given** the end of the chapter, **When** Pip successfully gets the glowing flower, **Then** the reader should understand that following the correct steps (algorithm) leads to solving the problem.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

-   **FR-001**: The chapter MUST be titled "The River of Algorithms".
-   **FR-002**: The chapter MUST feature Pip and Professor Hoot.
-   **FR-003**: The chapter MUST introduce the "River of Algorithms" as a metaphor for algorithms.
-   **FR-004**: The story MUST involve a clear goal (getting a glowing flower) and a clear set of steps (following specific currents) to achieve it.
-   **FR-005**: Professor Hoot MUST explain that this set of steps is called an "algorithm".
-   **FR-006**: The total word count MUST be between 400 and 500 words.

### Illustration Ideas

-   **Scene 1**: An illustration of the "River of Algorithms" with glowing, colorful currents flowing in different, complex directions.
-   **Scene 2**: Pip and Professor Hoot standing on a cliff overlooking the river, with Professor Hoot pointing towards a specific path of currents.
-   **Scene 3**: A map-like view of the river, showing the start point, the end point (the glowing flower on a cliff), and the correct sequence of currents to follow.
-   **Scene 4**: Pip successfully reaching the flower, looking proud.

## Success Criteria

### Measurable Outcomes

-   **SC-001**: A 9-year-old reader can explain that an "algorithm" is like a recipe or a set of instructions to do something.
-   **SC-002**: The final text adheres to the 400-500 word count.
-   **SC-003**: The chapter successfully builds on the previous chapter by showing how the "magic" of AI can be learned and used.
