# Feature Specification: Chapter 4 - The Forest of Decisions

**Feature Branch**: `004-chapter-four-the-forest-of-decisions`
**Created**: 2025-11-26
**Status**: Draft

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - Navigating the Fog (Priority: P1)

As a young reader, I want to follow Pip and Golem as they solve a series of simple questions to navigate a magical fog, so I can understand how complex problems can be solved by breaking them down into smaller decisions.

**Why this priority**: This chapter introduces the concept of neural networks and decision trees, which are key components of modern AI.

**Independent Test**: The chapter can be read on its own and successfully introduces the concept of a decision-making process using a series of yes/no questions.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1.  **Given** a reader starting the chapter, **When** they encounter the foggy forest and the glowing gates, **Then** they should understand that the gates represent a series of choices.
2.  **Given** the end of the chapter, **When** the fog clears, **Then** the reader should understand that a complex problem was solved by answering many simple questions in a row, similar to how a neural network functions.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

-   **FR-001**: The chapter MUST be titled "The Forest of Decisions".
-   **FR-002**: The chapter MUST feature Pip and the now-awakened Golem. Professor Hoot can also be present.
-   **FR-003**: The plot MUST revolve around navigating a magical, thick fog in the Whispering Woods.
-   **FR-004**: The navigation MUST be done by answering a series of simple yes/no questions at magical glowing gates.
-   **FR-005**: The concept of a "Neural Network" or "Decision Tree" MUST be explained as a path made of many small decisions.
-   **FR-006**: Successfully navigating the forest and clearing the fog MUST be the resolution of the chapter's conflict.
-   **FR-007**: The total word count MUST be between 400 and 500 words.

### Illustration Ideas

-   **Scene 1**: The Whispering Woods shrouded in a thick, magical, glowing fog, with Pip and Golem looking concerned.
-   **Scene 2**: Pip and Golem standing before a glowing gate. A simple yes/no question (e.g., "Is the sun to the West?") is floating in the air.
-   **Scene 3**: An illustration showing a branching path of light being created through the fog as they answer questions at multiple gates, representing the "decision tree".
-   **Scene 4**: Pip and Golem reaching the "heart of the forest," a giant, ancient tree, causing the fog to swirl and dissipate.

## Success Criteria

### Measurable Outcomes

-   **SC-001**: A 9-year-old reader can explain that the fog was cleared by making a lot of small "yes or no" choices.
-   **SC-002**: The final text adheres to the 400-500 word count.
-   **SC-003**: The chapter successfully introduces the concept of a neural network or decision tree in an accessible way.
