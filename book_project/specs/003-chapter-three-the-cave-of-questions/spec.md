# Feature Specification: Chapter 3 - The Cave of Questions

**Feature Branch**: `003-chapter-three-the-cave-of-questions`
**Created**: 2025-11-26
**Status**: Draft

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - Waking the Golem (Priority: P1)

As a young reader, I want to help Pip and Professor Hoot teach a sleeping golem how to answer questions, so I can understand the basic concept of machine learning through examples.

**Why this priority**: This chapter introduces the core concept of machine learning (training with data), which is a major part of AI.

**Independent Test**: The chapter can be read on its own and successfully introduces the concept of learning from question-and-answer pairs.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1.  **Given** a reader starting the chapter, **When** they see the golem and the crystals, **Then** they should understand that the crystals are examples for the golem to learn from.
2.  **Given** the end of the chapter, **When** the golem wakes up and answers a *new* question, **Then** the reader should understand that the golem has "learned" how to think for itself based on the examples it was shown.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

-   **FR-001**: The chapter MUST be titled "The Cave of Questions".
-   **FR-002**: The chapter MUST feature Pip and Professor Hoot.
-   **FR-003**: The chapter MUST introduce a new character: a sleeping golem.
-   **FR-004**: The story MUST involve teaching the golem by showing it examples of questions and answers on glowing crystals (supervised learning).
-   **FR-005**: The golem MUST wake up after being "trained" with enough examples.
-   **FR-006**: The awakened golem MUST be able to answer a new question it has never seen before.
-   **FR-007**: The total word count MUST be between 400 and 500 words.

### Illustration Ideas

-   **Scene 1**: The entrance to the dark, mysterious "Cave of Questions," with Pip and Professor Hoot looking inside.
-   **Scene 2**: A large, sleeping golem made of rock in the center of the cave. In front of it is a pile of glowing crystals.
-   **Scene 3**: A close-up of Pip holding a glowing crystal. One side shows a question mark, the other side shows an exclamation mark, representing the question/answer pair.
-   **Scene 4**: The golem beginning to wake up, with glowing patterns appearing on its rocky body, similar to the story-trees.
-   **Scene 5**: The fully awakened golem, looking friendly, as Pip asks it a new question.

## Success Criteria

### Measurable Outcomes

-   **SC-001**: A 9-year-old reader can explain that the golem learned by "studying" the crystal examples.
-   **SC-002**: The final text adheres to the 400-500 word count.
-   **SC-003**: The chapter successfully introduces the concept of machine learning in a simple, magical way.
