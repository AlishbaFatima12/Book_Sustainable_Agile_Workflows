# Feature Specification: Chapter 1 - The Whispering Woods of Data

**Feature Branch**: `001-chapter-one-the-whispering-woods`
**Created**: 2025-11-26
**Status**: Draft

## User Scenarios & Testing

### User Story 1 - A Curious Creature's Discovery (Priority: P1)

As a young reader, I want to follow a curious creature named Pip on an adventure into a magical forest, so that I can be introduced to the idea of "data" in a fun and engaging way.

**Why this priority**: This is the foundational story that introduces the core character and the central metaphor for data.

**Independent Test**: The chapter can be read on its own and successfully introduces Pip and the concept of the "Whispering Woods" where trees hold stories (data).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1.  **Given** a reader starting the book, **When** they read Chapter 1, **Then** they should understand that the forest's trees store information like a memory.
2.  **Given** the end of the chapter, **When** the reader meets the wise old creature, **Then** they should get a hint that this "magic" is something that can be learned, setting up future chapters.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

-   **FR-001**: The chapter MUST be titled "The Whispering Woods of Data".
-   **FR-002**: The chapter MUST introduce a main character named Pip, who is small and curious.
-   **FR-003**: The chapter MUST introduce a magical location: the "Whispering Woods," where trees store and share stories (data).
-   **FR-004**: The chapter MUST feature a wise older creature who provides a simple explanation of the woods' magic.
-   **FR-005**: The narrative MUST explain the concept of learning from data through the metaphor of listening to the trees' stories.
-   **FR-006**: The total word count MUST be between 400 and 500 words.

### Illustration Ideas

-   **Scene 1**: Pip, a small, glowing, and curious-looking creature, peering into the entrance of the dense, mystical Whispering Woods.
-   **Scene 2**: A close-up of the bark of a "story-tree," which has intricate, glowing patterns that swirl and shift. Pip is touching the tree with a look of wonder.
-   **Scene 3**: A wise old owl, wearing a pair of spectacles, perched on a branch, looking down at Pip and explaining the magic of the woods.

## Success Criteria

### Measurable Outcomes

-   **SC-001**: A 9-year-old reader can explain in their own words that the trees are like "living libraries."
-   **SC-002**: The final text adheres to the 400-500 word count.
-   **SC-003**: The chapter successfully establishes the main character and the core setting for the rest of the book.
